# Credit Care

An installable web app (PWA) to track, repair, and maintain your credit.

- Dashboard: scores by bureau and model with a trend chart, a credit health checklist, and upcoming reminders.
- Accounts: cards and loans (last 4 digits only), automatic utilization with color bands, and a pay-down calculator.
- Report Review: a guided checklist for each bureau, a log of negative items, and when each one ages off (FCRA §605).
- Disputes: a tracker with 30-day investigation countdowns (FCRA §611), plus links to the official bureau dispute pages.
- Letter Writer: bureau and furnisher dispute letters (FTC/CFPB sample structure), goodwill letters, and pay-for-delete requests. Copy, print, or save as PDF.
- Repair Plan: next steps, a snowball vs. avalanche payoff planner, and an on-time payment tracker.
- Maintenance: monthly, quarterly, and yearly routines, a credit freeze and fraud alert guide, and credit repair scam warnings.
- Learn: the FICO Score factors in plain English, with sources.
- Sync: optional, through a private Gist ("Credit Care Sync", credit-data.json) on the user's own GitHub account, using a gist-scope token that the user pastes in.
- Backup: export and import JSON. Backups never include the sync token.

Privacy: all data stays in the browser's localStorage. There is no analytics. The app never asks for an SSN, full account or card numbers, PINs, passwords, or bank logins.

Credit Care is educational only and is not legal or financial advice.
